### 🎮 Featured Project: SOUL REALM – Trial of Rebirth (Roguelike)

> **A fallen mage. A shifting soul dimension. Every death, a new beginning.**
>
> _Soul Realm: Trial of Rebirth_ is a fast-paced roguelike action game — fight through mystical, unstable worlds, master spell-based combat, and build broken-yet-overpowered spell builds with every run. **Death is only a new chance to get stronger!**

**Gameplay loop:**
- 🌌 Enter the ever-changing Soul Realm (broken temples, voids, floating islands)
- 🧠 Survive trials, fight waves, defeat bosses
- ⚡ All magic combat: cast spells, dash, and unleash special powers
- 🧬 Level up mid-run, mutate your build, go glass cannon or tanky sorcerer
- 🔁 Die? No ragequit! Rebirth and unlock new spells/abilities for next run

> **Unique mechanic:** Draw/select spells that EVOLVE as you progress — like Fireball → Split Fireball → Meteor Storm, unlocking wild combos mid-run. Every death brings you home to the Rebirth Hub to upgrade (permanently!) before trying again.

**Bosses, permadeath, chaos, and satisfying meta-progression — and that juicy game feel.**
